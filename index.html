Privacy Policy

FarmAIr is farm operations software. We collect the account and field data needed to run the product, keep it on our infrastructure, and delete it when you ask.

Last updated 24 September 2026.

Who we are

FarmAIr is operated by Obsydian Technologies Pty Ltd for commercial farms, starting in Zimbabwe. This policy covers the website at farmair.farm and the FarmAIr iOS and Android apps.

Data we collect

We collect only what the product needs to run a farm account:

  • Account data: name, email address, and a user identifier.
  • Farm data you enter: fields, budgets, activity, scouting notes, and photos.
  • Location: approximate and precise location when you map fields or log work from a phone.
  • Device identifiers used to deliver push notifications you opt into.

Why we use it

We use this data to authenticate you, store your farm records, show maps and photos, send operational notifications, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Access is limited to people on your farm account and to infrastructure providers that host the service (AWS). Payment processors are not used in the mobile apps today. We share data if the law requires it.

Retention and security

Account and farm records stay for as long as the farm uses FarmAIr. Data is encrypted in transit. When you request deletion we remove the account and associated farm records from production systems, except records we must keep for legal or security reasons.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your FarmAIr account and the farm data tied to it. Email hello@farmair.farm from the address on the account and ask us to delete the account, specific records, or both. We will action a deletion request within 30 days.

If you only want some data removed (for example a field, photo, or scouting note) say so in the same email. Signed-in users can also remove records inside the FarmAIr apps.
