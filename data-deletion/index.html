Request data deletion

You can ask FarmAIr to delete specific data tied to your account without closing the whole account.

Email hello@farmair.farm from the address on the account. Say which data to remove, for example a field, scouting note, photo, or activity record. We will action the request within 30 days.

Signed-in users can also delete many records directly inside the FarmAIr web and mobile apps.

To delete the entire account, see account deletion. Read our privacy policy.
